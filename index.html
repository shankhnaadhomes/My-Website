About Shankhnaad

A township built on sacred ground, designed for luxury living

Shankhnaad rises on Laksar Road, minutes from Har Ki Pauri, where the Ganga has drawn pilgrims and seekers for millennia. We asked a simple question: what if a residential address could hold both stillness and stature? The answer is a master-planned township of wide avenues, temple courtyards and manicured gardens — engineered for appreciation, built for belonging.

Haridwar's tourism economy, improving rail and road connectivity, and its standing as one of India's four Kumbh cities make it one of the more compelling under-the-radar plays in North Indian real estate today. Shankhnaad is positioned at the intersection of that spiritual permanence and long-term capital growth.

FAQ

Common questions

What plot sizes are available at Shankhnaad?+
Plots are available from 100 sq.yd up to 1000 sq.yd, allowing buyers to choose based on budget and intended use, from a compact second home to a large family estate.
Is Shankhnaad RERA registered?+
Yes. Shankhnaad is developed in accordance with applicable RERA regulations. Registration details are shared with every prospective buyer during the site visit and documentation process.
What is the payment plan structure?+
A flexible booking-plus-instalment structure is available, with booking amounts starting at 10% and balance payments spread over 6 to 48 months. Use the calculator above to estimate your own plan.
How far is Shankhnaad from Har Ki Pauri?+
Shankhnaad is approximately 12 minutes by road from Har Ki Pauri, keeping daily Ganga darshan within easy reach for residents.
Can NRIs purchase a plot at Shankhnaad?+
Yes. NRIs can purchase residential plots subject to standard FEMA guidelines. Our team assists with documentation, remote booking and power-of-attorney processes.
